# Control-area load forecasts with hierarchical reconciliation

_Generated by `gridmind control-areas`. Method chosen on the validation year by a rule fixed in advance (lowest mean MAE across all five series); the test year was evaluated once._

Each of the four German TSO control areas and Germany as a whole gets its own day-ahead load forecast (same model and issue-time contract as the national DE-LU model, with weather from the population points inside each area). Independent forecasts do not add up, so they are reconciled: bottom-up, OLS projection, or MinT with a shrunk error covariance.

**Chosen method: mint_shrink.** Base forecasts were incoherent by 518 MW on average (|Germany − sum of areas|); the TSOs' own forecasts by 0 MW.

- On the test year **bottom_up** was best, 4 MW lower in mean MAE than the chosen mint_shrink. The choice is kept as made on validation data; the gap is small.
- The shrinkage intensity is about 1: error correlations were too noisy to estimate reliably, so MinT reduced to variance weighting (WLS). Germany's forecast has the largest error variance, so it is adjusted most, which is why MinT ends up close to bottom-up.
