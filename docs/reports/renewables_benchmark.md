# Day-ahead renewables forecasts (DE-LU): benchmark

_Generated by `gridmind renewables` from `renewables_results.json`._

**Setup.**
- Issue time D-1 10:00 Europe/Berlin, the same as the load forecast. Weather: GFS forecasts made 48 h before each hour (Open-Meteo).
- **v1** used the 13 population points (radiation, cloud, 10 m wind). **v2** adds 16 points at solar, onshore-wind and offshore sites with 100 m wind, radiation, cloud and temperature.
- Model: quantile gradient boosting with conformal calibration. The target is either generation ÷ the maximum output of the previous 30 days ("ratio") or generation in MW ("absolute").
- Per technology, the variant was chosen on the validation period by a rule fixed in advance (lowest mean pinball loss). **The test year had already been evaluated once for v1**, so v2's test result is a second look at the same year and is reported as such.
- Benchmark: the TSOs' day-ahead generation forecasts (SMARD). **They are published at about 18:00 the day before, 8 hours after GridMind's issue time**, with fresher weather and dedicated models.

Validation 2025-05-01 → 2025-10-31; test year 2025-11-01 → 2026-10-31.

## Reading the result

Weather at the right sites and height matters most for wind, where 10 m wind at population centres was a poor proxy. The remaining gap to the TSO forecasts comes from their later issue time, their dedicated models and their knowledge of installed capacity and curtailment. The intervals stay calibrated, so the model's uncertainty is honest even where its point forecasts are worse.
